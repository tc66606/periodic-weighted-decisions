# Third-party materials and dependencies

Python, mpmath, NumPy and SciPy are installed separately; implementations and
environments are not vendored. Their upstream distributions provide their own
licences. Version pins do not replace those licences.

Gate-synthesis records include outputs produced with Ross-Selinger/gridsynth
methods cited in the manuscript. No gridsynth executable or library is bundled.
The author has confirmed permissions for copied third-party materials and code.
Author-owned code is MIT; original data, certificates and documentation are
CC BY 4.0. Any third-party terms continue to apply to those materials and take
precedence over the author-owned material classification.

No literature PDFs or font binaries are included. This inventory describes the
archive contents and is not an independent copyright audit.
